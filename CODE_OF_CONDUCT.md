# Community Code of Conduct

SharBo Globo aims to keep public collaboration professional, constructive and technically focused.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and in good faith;
- critique ideas and code rather than people;
- provide reproducible technical information where possible;
- respect privacy, copyright, licenses and confidential information;
- avoid publishing production source intelligence or credentials in issues, discussions or pull requests;
- accept maintainer decisions about project scope, licensing, branding and public/private boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption, doxxing, credential disclosure, malicious source-data disclosure, impersonation, or knowingly submitting material without the right to do so are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content; close contributions; restrict participation; or take other reasonable moderation actions when behavior threatens contributors, the project, its legal obligations or its security boundaries.

For sensitive conduct matters, contact the maintainer privately using the contact method on the repository owner's GitHub profile rather than posting personal or confidential details publicly.

## Scope

This policy applies to official SharBo Globo project spaces and interactions undertaken on behalf of the project.
